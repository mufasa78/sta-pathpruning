## 🎯 **CircuitNet Dataset - Available on Hugging Face**

### What it is:
CircuitNet is an open-source dataset for machine learning applications in Electronic Design Automation (EDA), specifically designed for VLSI CAD tasks.

### Key Features:

**Two Versions Available:**

1. **CircuitNet-N28** (Original)
   - Includes timing features for circuit analysis
   - Covers routability, timing, and power data from the design flow

2. **CircuitNet-N14** (Newer)
   - Currently maintained on Hugging Face
   - Supports congestion, IR drop and timing prediction
   - Contains more than 10,000 samples with a variety of chip designs including CPU, GPU, and AI Chip
   - All designs conducted through complete commercial design flows in 14nm FinFET technology

### What You'll Get:

- **LEF/DEF files** (circuit layout data)
- **Netlist information**
- **Graph features** (perfect for your endpoint-based graph construction!)
- **Timing features** (exactly what you need for STA!)
- **Pretrained weights** for various tasks

### Perfect for Your Project Because:

✅ **Timing data included** - You need this for training your anchor prediction model
